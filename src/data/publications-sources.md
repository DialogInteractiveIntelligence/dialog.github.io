  once under their published DOI. Earlier arXiv/SSRN versions are not separate
  papers. Linked arXiv PDFs are labelled as preprints when a published version exists.
- The two identical Expert entries and duplicate Sequence Matters entries on
  Manuel's profile are consolidated.
- [How Explanation Cues Prevent Decay of Established Knowledge in Verbal Interactions](https://osf.io/akz4c)
  is an OSF study preregistration. The [researcher's mission report](https://lithme.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/LITHME-STSM-2023-report-Robrecht.pdf)
  describes the study preregistration and data collection. It is not listed as a paper.

  is authored by M.E. Ayoub; TU Delft identifies Ruben and Catharine as mentors.
  The other supervised bachelor theses on Ruben's profile are likewise excluded.

  has Paul as its sole author, so it does not qualify.
