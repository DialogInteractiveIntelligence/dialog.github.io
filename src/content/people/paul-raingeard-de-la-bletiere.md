Paul Raingeard de la Blétière is a PhD candidate in the Interactive Intelligence Group at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft), supervised by Catharine Oertel and Mark Neerincx. 
Holding a Master's background in robotics engineering and computer science from Keio University, Ecole Centrale Nantes and University of Genoa, his research centers on human-robot interaction and assistive social technologies for people with dementia. As part of the NWO-funded QoLEAD consortium, he co-designs generative AI-driven musical robots with care recipients and healthcare staff to facilitate music co-creation, personalized singing, and memory-based recommendations that reduce stress and improve quality of life.
