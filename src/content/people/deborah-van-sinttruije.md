Deborah is a PhD candidate in the Interactive Intelligence group at TU Delft. Her research focuses on human–AI collaboration, with a particular interest in how intelligent systems can reason about human memory and other cognitive states under uncertainty. She uses methods from reinforcement learning, POMDPs, and cognitive science to develop adaptive agents that can better support individual users.

Prior to her PhD, Deborah obtained a MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and a MSc in Applied Cognitive Psychology at Universiteit Leiden.
