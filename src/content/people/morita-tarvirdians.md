Morita's research focuses on dialog modeling for conversational agents that support reflection before high-stakes personal decisions. She develops agents that maintain an explicit model of the reflection as it unfolds, tracking which considerations a person has explored and how far each has been developed, and use this model to decide how to steer the conversation. Her recent work studies how these steering decisions can be shared between agent and user, where the agent surfaces what it observes in the interaction, and the user supplies the reasons behind it.

This work is realised in ReflectiMate, published at UMAP 2026 and EMNLP 2026 Findings. 
She will continue this line of research as a postdoctoral researcher with Catharine Oertel.
